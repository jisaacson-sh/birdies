Rules
  • Gross birdies or better in home tournaments at Rancho Park count. Birdies are read from Golf Genius scorecards after each round.
  • Each player starts with credit on the lowest-handicap holes: half of their locked Low Index course handicap, rounded up.
  • First player to birdie all 18 holes wins. Otherwise the leader after the last home tournament of the season takes the pot.
